Parking

You can park in the surrounding streets, for example in Smrčínská Street, which is just around the corner.

Important information

Phone contact is intended only for urgent situations (such as being late or last-minute cancellation) and is available from 9:00 to 19:00. If I do not answer, I am most likely in session and will return your call as soon as possible.

If you have any questions about whether therapy is suitable for you or your child, or if you would like to clarify your consultation or booking, please do not hesitate to contact me via email: terapieucaje@email.cz.
